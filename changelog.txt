Version: 0.2.1
Date: ????
  Changes:
    - Changes to support Artisanal Reskins: Assets API 0.2.1.
    - Dying explosions, particles, and remnants now come from the Artisanal Reskins: Assets API, with the sprites of each entity, and remnants now have the size of the entity.
    - The in-world pictures of the items of reskinned entities, equipment, and robot frames now have mipmaps.
    - The composite recipe icons of the heat pipe, valve, and pipe recipes no longer show the tier labels of the items they show.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed that reskinned robots did not play the death sounds of the base game.
    - Fixed that a checkpoint technology with a single icon file and no background failed to load.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-01
  Features:
    - Updated for Factorio 2.1.
    - Tier labels may now be shown in world, or not, based on the "Show tier labels in world" setting.
    - Added initial support for heat pipes.
    - Added support for the 4th tier storage tank.
    - Added support for beacons, hangars, relays, labs, chargers, drone launchers, and others.
    - Added support for equipment and equipment-adjacent icons, recipes, and technologies, including leg augmentations, night vision, generator equipment, self-repair packs.
    - Added reskinned technology and checkpoint graphics for labs, large tanks, thermal tanks, mining drills, heat pipes, Stirling engines, and others.
  Changes:
    - Revised support for combustion chambers and boilers:
      - Fire and fire glow animations now work correctly.
      - Combustion chambers and boilers now use different icons; as such, boiler tiers are now independent and are 2 and 3 when using progression.
    - Open and Closed turbines are now presented as independent tiers from 1 to 3. Icons now include a symbol to distinguish the open and closed variants.
      - Note: the in-world entities are not yet visually distinct except when running.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed that some items would show tier labels when on belts and on the ground, unintentionally.
    - Fixed that pipes and underground pipes did not respect the "Show tier labels on pipes" setting.
    - Fixed that the Pipe 1 (stone) and Pipe 2 (plastic) recipes were inconsistent with the other recipe and item icons.
